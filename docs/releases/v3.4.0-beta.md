## New: Game Filter
Color filters for Roblox, like NVIDIA's game filters – a new page in the sidebar. Windows applies them to the picture on your screen, so Roblox itself is never touched and there's no risk for your accounts.

- **Brightness / Contrast** – exposure, brightness, contrast
- **Color** – tint color and intensity, temperature, saturation
- **Look** – black & white, sepia / old film, night mode and three color blind modes, each with a strength slider
- **Frame** – vignette (dark edges) and letterbox (cinema bars)
- **Profiles** – Vivid, Cinematic, Night, Black & white and Retro to start with, plus your own
- **Live preview** – see every change right away, or preview it on the whole screen
- **Hotkeys, also in game** – Ctrl + Alt + F turns the filter on or off, Ctrl + Alt + G switches profiles
- Only active while a Roblox window is in front – switch to Discord or your browser and it's gone
- Settings in sections you open and close, like NVIDIA's filter panel

## Enhanced mode
Orbit's own GPU filter on top, for everything a simple color filter can't do:
- **Highlights, shadows and gamma**
- **Vibrance** – dull colors get more color than already colorful ones
- **Details** – sharpen, clarity, HDR toning and bloom
- Adds about one frame of delay, so it's a switch of its own

## Digital vibrance (NVIDIA)
On NVIDIA graphics cards Orbit turns up the driver's digital vibrance while you play – it costs no performance, and your own setting comes back the moment you leave Roblox.
