## New
- **Animated orbit background** on the Play page – nebula, stars, glowing orbits with moons around your avatar, reacts to the mouse, shock wave on launch (can be turned off)
- **Disconnect notification** – tells you which account lost its game and why (error 277, kicked, server closed)
- **What's new** window once after every update
- **Robux balance** in the account picker
- **Login check** at start – shows which accounts have to log in again
- **Playtime** – today, this week and total on the Play page
- **Launch presets** – save accounts + game with a name and launch everything with one click
- **Arrange windows** – put all Roblox windows together with one click, and back again
- **Resource saver** – Roblox windows in the background get less CPU, the one you play in stays fast
- Orbit logo in the name tag
