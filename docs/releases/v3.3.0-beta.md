## New: Account overview
A new page in the sidebar that shows every open Roblox window live, on one screen.

- **Live tiles** – each Roblox window as a live picture, drawn by Windows itself, so it costs almost no performance
- **At a glance** – account name and picture, the game it's playing (or "Disconnected"), CPU and RAM usage and whether the resource saver slows it down
- **One click** – click a tile to bring that window to the front, or close it right from the tile
- **Tile size** – small, medium or large, so 20+ windows fit on one screen
- **Arrange & put back** – the window arranging from the Accounts page, right next to your tiles
- **Hide names** – hides all account names and pictures, for screenshots and streams

## Stronger resource saver
- **Max CPU per window** – Roblox windows in the background can be limited to 2, 5, 10 or 25 % of your CPU (Roblox's frame rate drops with it, the game keeps running). Windows that already use less aren't affected
- **Efficiency mode** – Windows runs background Roblox windows on its power-saving cores
- The window you play in is never limited, and all limits are removed when you turn the saver off or close Orbit

## Anti-AFK (optional, off by default)
- Presses Space in every Roblox window every 5, 10 or 15 minutes, so Roblox doesn't kick you after 20 minutes of no input
- Each window comes to the front for a moment and gets a real key press – only while you're away from mouse and keyboard, then your window gets the focus back
- **Test** button to try it right away
