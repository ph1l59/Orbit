## New
- **Live friend notifications** – Orbit keeps a live connection to Roblox (like the website), so you're told right away when a friend comes online or starts playing
- **Friend request notifications** – new requests pop up immediately, one click opens the friends panel
- Green **LIVE** badge in the friends panel while the live connection is up

## Safety net
- If the live connection drops, Orbit reconnects by itself and still checks every 30 seconds in the meantime
