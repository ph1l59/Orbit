## New: PC Tweaks
A new page in the sidebar with tools that keep your PC fast while you play.

### RAM cleaner
- **See your memory at a glance** – used, free and total RAM with a live bar and a status (Healthy · High · Needs cleaning)
- **Clean with one click** – apps you're not using give back memory they don't need, so your game has more room
- **The game you play is never touched** – neither is the window you were in before opening Orbit
- **Allow list** – add apps that should never be cleaned
- **Automatic cleaning** – when your RAM goes above 85 %, or every 30 / 60 minutes
- **Multi-Roblox friendly** – optionally include Roblox windows running in the background

### DNS optimizer
- **Tests popular DNS servers** (Cloudflare, Google, Quad9, OpenDNS, AdGuard, Control D, Comodo) right from your PC
- **Ranked by speed** – see the average response time of each server and of the DNS you use now
- **Choose fastest** – switch Windows to the fastest DNS with one click (Windows asks for permission once)
- **Reset to automatic** – go back to your router's DNS at any time

## Improved
- **Arrange windows works with fullscreen** – Roblox windows in fullscreen are taken out of it first, so every window has its title bar again to move, minimize or close it
- **Put back** returns those windows to fullscreen
