## Orbit 3.0
### Play page
- New **start page**: pick an account and launch the Roblox app with one click
- **Turnable 3D avatar** – drag to turn, flick to spin, double-click to reset
- New launch button and **loading window** while Roblox starts

### Friends
- **Friends panel** that slides in from the right: online status, search, one-click **Join**
- **Friend requests** – accept, decline and send requests with a badge on the button
- **Privacy settings** of your account right inside Orbit (online status, who can join, inventory, trades, …)

### Other
- The download is now called **Orbit-Launcher.exe**
