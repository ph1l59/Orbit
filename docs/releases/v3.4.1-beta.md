## New: Outfits on the Play page
- **Outfits button** next to Launch – shows all outfits you saved on Roblox, each with the whole avatar
- One click puts the outfit on your account; Orbit reloads your 3D avatar and your account picture
- A notification tells you when the outfit changed

## Launching
- **New delays between accounts:** "No wait (all at once)" and "1 second"
- **Clearer errors:** the reason is shown right under the launch button, not only on the account card
- If Roblox is limiting launches or wants a security check, Orbit now says so and what to do

## Fixes
- **Auto clicker:** the hotkey now also works while you hold other keys, like W, Shift or Ctrl while running in game
- **Account pictures:** no more empty pictures while Roblox is still drawing a new avatar – Orbit waits and asks again
- **Avatar:** waits longer for Roblox to finish drawing, and "click to try again" if it still isn't there
- The picture in the friends panel updates right away after an outfit change
