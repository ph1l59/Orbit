Accounts

The Roblox account manager that just works

Stop logging in and out. Orbit keeps all your Roblox accounts in one place and launches any of them – or all of them – with one click.

Download Orbit – free All features

How it works

  1. Download and open Orbit

    Free for Windows 10 and 11, no sign-up.

  2. Add an account

    Click Add account. A window with the real roblox.com login opens – log in like always, including 2-step verification.

  3. Organise

    Give accounts an alias, drag them into your order and see at a glance who is online or in a game.

  4. Launch

    Pick an account on the Play page and press Launch, or select several on the Accounts page and join a game together.

What you get

  • Logins stay on your PC, encrypted with Windows' own protection – nothing is sent to us
  • Login check at start: Orbit tells you which accounts have to log in again
  • Robux balance and online status of every account
  • Open roblox.com in a browser that's already logged in as the account
  • Join games, private servers, share links or follow a player – with one or many accounts
  • Create new accounts faster: birthday and gender once, then a list of usernames

Questions

Is it safe to add my account to Orbit?

You log in on the real roblox.com page, not in a form made by Orbit. Orbit only keeps the login session, encrypted with Windows (DPAPI) – it can only be read by your Windows user on your PC. Orbit is open source, so anyone can check the code.

Does Orbit save my password?

No. Orbit keeps the login session Roblox gives you after logging in. If it expires, Orbit tells you and you simply log in again.

Can I use it with 2-step verification?

Yes. The login happens on Roblox's own page, so 2-step verification works exactly like in your browser.

What happens if I share the accounts file?

Never share accounts.dat or send it to anyone. Whoever has it on your PC could use your logins.
