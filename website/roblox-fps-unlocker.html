How it works

  1. Download and open Orbit

    Free for Windows 10 and 11.

  2. Open Global settings

    Go to Global Settings in the sidebar.

  3. Set your frame rate limit

    Enter the FPS you want under Framerate limit – for example your monitor's refresh rate (144, 165, 240 …). Above 240 FPS isn't recommended, it can cause physics and ping issues.

  4. Save and keep it

    Click Save. Turn on Set as read-only so Roblox doesn't reset your settings. Restart Roblox to see the change.

  5. Optional: more FPS with FastFlags

    On the FastFlags page, lower mesh detail, turn off anti-aliasing (MSAA) or pick another rendering mode for extra frames on weaker PCs.

What you get

  • Frame rate limit, graphics quality, sensitivity and more in one place
  • Read-only mode so Roblox doesn't overwrite your settings
  • FastFlag presets that Roblox still allows (only allowlisted flags are applied since September 2025)
  • Full FastFlag editor with JSON import and export
  • Resource saver for when you run several Roblox windows

Questions

Is an FPS unlocker allowed in Roblox?

Orbit doesn't inject anything into the game. It changes Roblox's own settings file and only applies FastFlags that Roblox itself allows.

Why does my FPS not change?

Restart Roblox after saving – the settings are read when Roblox starts. Also check that VSync or a frame limit in your graphics driver isn't capping it.

What FPS should I choose?

Your monitor's refresh rate is a good choice (for example 144 on a 144 Hz monitor). More FPS than your monitor shows only adds heat and power use.

Do FastFlags still work?

Since September 2025 Roblox only applies FastFlags from its allowlist. Orbit's presets only use allowed flags – other flags are simply ignored by Roblox.
